The three model types

How LLMs, VLMs and SLMs work, what they cost, where they run, and how leading firms combine them

A language model does one thing: it predicts the next token, again and again

1  The text is cut into tokens

A small model is the same design with fewer parameters, prepared for one job

Distillation

A large model answers thousands of questions, and the small model is trained to give the same answers.

Fine-tuning

Your own solved cases teach it one task. One study of financial tables needed 2,152 examples.

Quantisation

14 GB

3.5 GB

Each parameter is stored in 4 bits instead of 16, so a 7B model shrinks to a quarter of the memory.

What you give up is breadth. Outside its own task a small model gets worse quickly, so each one needs its own test set and its own owner.

Memory is for the weights alone: 7 billion parameters at 2 bytes and at half a byte each. Fine-tuning example: arXiv 2508.05669

An agent is a model working in a loop with tools, and the three models take different roles

Plan

decide the next step

Act

call a tool or system

Read

take in what came back

Check

done, retry, or ask a person

Large model: the planner

Breaks the goal into steps, chooses what to do next, and deals with the case nobody expected.

Small models: the workers

Do the narrow steps that repeat thousands of times: pick the tool, fill in its fields, classify the reply.

Vision model: the eyes

Reads whatever comes back as a page, a scan or a screen, and turns it into fields the others can use.

Most steps in an agent are narrow and repeated. NVIDIA's researchers argue these belong on small models, at 10–30x lower serving cost.

Source: NVIDIA, Small Language Models are the Future of Agentic AI (2025)
