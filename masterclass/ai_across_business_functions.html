Agents earn autonomy in three levels, and most AMC work belongs at the first two

Level 1

Drafts

The model prepares; a person does everything after

Level 2

Acts after approval

The agent prepares the action; a person approves each one

Level 3

Acts within limits

The agent completes routine cases alone, inside set limits, with every action logged and sampled

Fits: research notes, commentary, rationale records

Fits: onboarding exceptions, distributor and investor replies

Fits: tagging and routing requests, opening compliance tasks, internal look-ups

A working rule: anything that reaches an investor or the order book stays at level 2 or below.

Today 7% of asset managers have deployed agents and 5% give AI any autonomous authority in investing.

The rule is our recommendation. Sources: EY (Sept 2025); Mercer (May 2026)

A function head brings four things to every use case

1

An owner

One named person accountable for the outcome, from the business and not from IT

2

A baseline

Today's cost, turnaround time and error rate for the process, measured before any build

3

200–500 real cases

Past items with the correct answer, so every model and every release is scored the same way

4

A checkpoint

The step where a person approves, and the cases that always go to a person

Without these four, a pilot cannot show a return. In Mercer's 2026 survey, 55% of managers use AI in an investment process and 8% report a measurable improvement.

Source: Mercer, AI in asset management survey of 131 managers (May 2026)

A thirteen-week plan takes one process in one function from choice to live use

Baseline

Weeks 1–2

Pick one process. Record today's cost, turnaround time and error rate.

Build and test

Weeks 3–6

Collect 200–500 solved cases. Try two or three models against them.

Run in shadow

Weeks 7–10

The model works alongside people; nothing it produces is used yet.

Go live with approval

Weeks 11–13

A person approves every output. Report the numbers to the leadership team.

The function head

owns the process, the baseline and the decision to go live

The platform team

supplies the models, the gateway and the logs

Risk and compliance

review the shadow-run results before anything reaches an investor

The timings are a planning guide. Shadow testing before launch is among the practices in SEBI's June 2025 draft.
